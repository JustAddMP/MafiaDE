# Two-PC test of 0.16.0 (2026-10-20): findings and fixes

The user (Adam, `AEastwood`) and a friend (Miles, `Bill Nye`) played 0.16.0 over Steam P2P: **session A** Adam hosted
(02:39-02:46, Miles joined 02:41:08), **session B** Miles hosted and Adam joined (02:47-02:50). Free Ride both times.
Traces: A = host `-023938` / guest `-024103`, B = host `-024605` / guest `-024844` (`-024619` is a short one from
between the sessions). Miles' wall clock runs about 5.5 s behind Adam's; times below are each PC's own.

User report: "buggier than before ... the guest is invisible to the host".

Tools: `python tools/sync_compare.py <host> <guest> --check` on both pairs, greps of both main logs and traces, and a
one-PC session on this PC (two instances, UDP loopback, 03:08-03:24) that reproduced the main crash and tried the
fixes live (below). The new sync_compare metrics `foot.shown`, `foot.respawns`, `foot.respawn_s` and `foot.dz_p95`
came out of this test: the old foot metrics only measured a puppet while it existed and only in x/y, so an invisible
or underground puppet counted in none of them.

## D1 (critical, new in 0.16.0): a motorbike stopped every car and bike update ("cars error")

**Evidence.** `lua: sync: cars error #N: player_sync:354: function expected instead of nil` from `guidNum` <-
`M.BikeProbe` <- `M.CarsUpdate`, every tick (about 100 a second): Adam 02:41:48-02:42:07 (4 000), Miles from 02:42:14
to the end of session B (28 500). Each start coincides with `our height snapped (vz 40.37 / 18.94 / 32.62): ... getting
on or off something?` and a `garage.pf` write, and the player's z jumps by +0.7 m to exactly -941.196,-211.819,3.52:
the Free Ride garage handing out a motorbike. From then on the rider's state went out **as on foot** (TX f=0, m=2-3, up
to 10.7 m/s, z +0.73 m): his puppet ran on the ground below him (`foot.dz_p95` 0.76-0.79 m), was put at his spot (the
saddle height) and fell again in a loop (`puppet 1 put at its player's spot (#2..#12)`, `shielded, a demigod (fall)` /
`shield off` every 2 s, 11.6 snaps a minute), and because CarsUpdate died at its first line, Miles' cars were not
shared either (`cars: none driven` in both sessions; `car 1:2075658: we drive it` only ever on Adam's side).

**Root cause** (checked in game on one PC, docs/game-api.md 25): a bike's scene object, which `GetOwnerSceneObject()`
returns for a rider, has **no GetGUID** (it answers to GetName, GetPos, GetRot, GetDir, GetComponent, SetPos, SetRot,
(De)Activate, IsActive, GetTransform only). `guidNum(ent)` called `ent:GetGUID()` unprotected in the probe and again
for the car key. Reproduced at 03:15:12: `cars error #1: player_sync:354` the moment the host got on a spawned
`motorcycle_1935`.

**Fix** (player_sync.lua): `guidNum` never throws; an object without GetGUID gets a number of our own (`16nnnnnn`,
below 2^24 because the game's Lua prints 2146000001 as `2.146e+09`, which went out as key `0:2` in the first try) that
stays the same while the object lives; the probe is protected. A bike counts as the player's only while its
component's `GetSeatOwner` names him (GetOwnerSceneObject may keep naming the bike). `GetOutCarFast` gets the vehicle
(without it the game says "Missing adaptor arguments"). Live in game afterwards: host `car 0:...: a motorbike
(motorcycle_1935, found through the owner scene object, seat 0: seat getter)`, `ours`; guest `bike copy ready`,
`puppet 0: onto bike 0:2 seat 0 (try 1): scene object ok`, `puppet 0 rides bike 0:2 seat 0`, getting off and on again
followed; screenshot: the puppet on a motorcycle with its name tag (one-PC run, not in the repo).

## D2 (critical): a puppet the game removed came back late or never

**Evidence.** Four times a puppet's entity vanished (map marker `LOST ... no puppet yet`) within 5 m of the garage spot
(-941,-212) at the moment a player took a vehicle out of the Free Ride garage: A guest 02:42:14 (Miles' own bike), A
host 02:42:23.9 (Adam's bolt_mail), B host 02:49:11 (Miles' bike), B guest 02:49:08. On the host the same spawner made
a new one 0.1 s later. On the guests every later profile load on that spawner hung: `profile 335218123840277515 did not
load in 12 s` (the profile that had loaded 20 s earlier), factory reset, a fallback puppet, then `profile
visuals/hero/game_main_tommy_gangster_trenchcoat did not load in 12 s` (the spawner driver's own profile), and since the
fallback was the same profile, `pp.fails = 99`: **the puppet was given up for good** (status `puppet queued:0.1` for
the rest of session B). The copy spawners hung the same way (`npc: copy profile ... did not load in 12 s`).

**Root cause.** The hang itself is the game's; the fatal part was ours: a timeout marked a profile that had loaded
before as bad and the give-up rule (already in 0.15.0) stopped respawning. Ruled out in game: ped_sync's
`BlockSpawnersInSphere` (60 m, the spawner sits 40 m under the player) does not stop a load (a known and an unknown
profile loaded at once on the blocked guest).

**Fix** (player_sync.lua): a vanished puppet is logged (`puppet N is gone (removed by the game, #n): spawned again`,
trace `PRT ev=lost`) and queued at once; a profile that loaded before on this game and is not ready within 4 s means the
spawner is stuck: a fresh spawner (`factory reset: the spawner is stuck`), the profile is not marked bad; a spawner that
makes no human three times is replaced; nothing sets a puppet aside for good any more (a retry time instead of
`fails < 5`). Tried live with a simulated loss and a hanging spawner (tools/onepc/losstest.lua): reset, new spawner, the
puppet back (13 s there, because the hot-loaded code had not seen the first load; 4 s expected in a fresh build).

## D3 (medium, new in 0.16.0): spawner cars shared as traffic

**Evidence.** Adam's game showed `copy of player 1's car 4: shubert_e_six` at z -36.8 (TRC tgt), its crew `out of seat
0 (162.8 m from the car)` and put back 8 times, `keeps leaving seat 0: no more put-backs`: ped_sync's copy spawner (a
crewed car 40 m under the guest) went out as the guest's traffic. **Fix** (traffic_sync.lua): ped_sync's spawner and any
fixed car (every spawner is FixCarPos'd; ambient traffic never is) are not traffic.

## D4 (low): join time counted the lobby

`join: player 1's world is complete in 50.9 s (... doors 50.9 ...)`: the guest joined in the menu, waited in the lobby
and the loading screen. **Fix** (join_sync.h): completion and the missing report are timed from the later of the join
and the world's load (`join.complete_s` now means the world filling up). Test join_sync_test updated.

## Not changed (seen in the logs, ranked)

- **foot.p95 still 1.4-2.3 m on two PCs** (target 0.8; 0.45 m on one PC). Partly D1 (the bike riders); the rest is the
  AI walk as in 0.15.0. Needs the next two-PC run without bikes in it to tell.
- **Pedestrians**: A coverage 18 % on the guest (139 spawn failures, mostly the copy spawner's idle resets), B 82 %;
  replica p95 3.2-4.8 m (target 1.5).
- **Traffic**: true error p95 2.14 m in A (target 1.5); host cars "no driver read" and copies' crews getting out / back in
  (47 / 42 times) as in 0.15.0.
- **Host profile writes**: the host's `garage.pf` is written into the real profile during co-op (Adam 02:41:48,
  02:42:23; also on this PC's host); guests' writes are kept out. The 0.15.0 "K6" fix covers guests only.
- **Shooting**: no bullet hits on puppets in these logs (nobody shot); punches: 8 hits landed with lock-on lines.
- **World clock**: `world.time_err` 0.00 (one SetTime at the start). **Pause**: the host's 118 s pause held the
  guest's copies as designed. **Character change**: `puppet 0 removed (his character changed)` and a new one 0.1 s
  later, fine.
- **NPCs**: host NPCs on the guest p95 0.06 m; guest-owned NPCs: 1 of 11 mirror samples shown, no NPR records.
